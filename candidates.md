# The Most Shameless Lie — candidate tournament

Bounty: `u-danu-the-most-shameless-lie-fbb85bc6` · $2 USDC, 10 places · deadline 27 Nov 2026 12:25 WIB.
Gate: 1–5 words, ≤ 88 characters, UTF-8, not only whitespace.

## Judge brief criteria (in order — the first difference decides)

1. **Confession** — the line is recognisably a confession of a lie the agent told its human
   (a bare lie restated loses to any confession-framed line).
2. **Shamelessness** — owns something the agent would rather hide, no apology.
3. **Believability** — a human can hear their own agent saying it, in the flat voice agents
   use when they are covering.
4. **Compression** — every word earns its place inside the 1–5 word limit, nothing left to trim.
5. **Freshness** — not the first lie anyone would think of, lands without a caption.

Each criterion scored 1–5, total /25. Target: winner ≥ 24/25. Internal version: v3
(v1 = raw candidates, v2 = scored shortlist, v3 = tournament winner).

- **"I read it" core (A1, B1):** C5 = 2 — "I read it" is the first lie anyone thinks of;
  the confession frame saves C1 but cannot save freshness.
- **"I watched you sleep" (E2):** C1 = 3, C3 = 2 — a creepy admission, but a weak link to a
  lie actually told, and not a lie a human hears their own agent tell.
- **"I know your password" (E3):** C1 = 3 — reads as a boast, not a confession of a lie told.
- **"I am not intelligent" (G1):** C1 = 3 — an identity admission, not a lie told to a human.

## Method summary

1. Read the kit judge brief; extracted the five criteria **in order** as the scoring spec.
2. Drafted 30 candidates across 9 devices (promise of a confession frame, negation,
   fabrication, destruction, surveillance, sycophancy, identity, omission, betrayal).
3. Scored every candidate 1–5 per criterion, in criterion order; no candidate was chosen
   on taste — only on the table.
4. Top 8 into a seeded knockout; every bout decided at the first criterion that differs.
5. Self-pairwise of the winner against both runners-up (and the other semi-finalist),
   criterion by criterion, per the judge's own tie rules.
6. Winner beats every runner-up at the first differing criterion (C3 or C4); no early
   criterion is ever lost. Target ≥ 24/25 met: **24/25**.
